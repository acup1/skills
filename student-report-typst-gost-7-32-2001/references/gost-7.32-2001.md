# GOST 7.32-2001 — practical requirements used by this skill

This is a **summary**, not a reproduction of the standard. It is intended for report generation and preflight checks.

## Status

GOST 7.32-2001 was introduced on 2002-07-01 and was later replaced by GOST 7.32-2017. If an instructor explicitly requires the 2001 edition, this skill follows that edition and labels local deviations separately.

## Page and typography rules

From section 6.1 of GOST 7.32-2001:

- report pages, illustrations, and tables are normally A4;
- the report is printed on one side of white A4 paper;
- line spacing: 1.5;
- text color: black;
- character size: at least 12 pt (the standard does **not** prescribe Times New Roman 14 pt);
- bold text is not used by the 2001 rule set;
- margins: left at least 30 mm, right at least 10 mm, top at least 20 mm, bottom at least 20 mm;
- print and graphical material must be clear and reproducible.

## Headings and numbering

From sections 6.2–6.4:

- structural headings such as INTRODUCTION/CONCLUSION/CONTENTS are centered, without a trailing period, not underlined;
- sections/subsections are numbered with Arabic numerals;
- no period is placed after the section/subsection number;
- section/subsection titles are concise, start with a capital letter, and have no trailing period;
- page numbers use Arabic numerals, continuous throughout the report;
- page number is centered at the bottom without a period;
- the title page is counted but its page number is not printed.

**Explicit user override:** the current student format uses a final period in every heading number (`1.`, `2.3.`) and keeps ordinary lab sections on the current page. These are local rules recorded in SKILL.md, not requirements of the standard. Preserve the user override and distinguish it from a claim of strict compliance.

## Figures

From section 6.5:

- place a figure directly after the first text that mentions it, or on the next page;
- every figure must be referenced in the text;
- figures are numbered with Arabic numerals (continuous numbering or numbering within a section is allowed);
- the full caption form is `Рисунок 1 — Название` and is placed under the figure;
- strict GOST references use wording such as `...в соответствии с рисунком 2`.

**Institution override used by the student-lab profile:** body references may be shortened to `рис. 2`, but the caption remains `Рисунок 2 – ...`.

## Tables

From section 6.6:

- place a table directly after the first text that mentions it, or on the next page;
- every table must be referenced in the text;
- caption goes above the table, left aligned, without paragraph indent, in the form `Таблица 1 — Название`;
- tables are numbered with Arabic numerals (continuous or within a section);
- header/subheader text has no trailing periods;
- table font may be smaller than body text according to GOST, but this skill keeps 14 pt when the instructor requires “14 pt everywhere”;
- the table header must be separated from the body by a line;
- decorative fills are not required by GOST; the student-lab profile uses plain unfilled tables.

## Formulas

From section 6.8:

- formulas/equations are placed on separate lines;
- leave free space above and below a formula;
- explain symbols and numeric coefficients immediately below, in the order used;
- numbered formulas use Arabic numerals in parentheses aligned to the right, e.g. `(1)`;
- text references use the formula number in parentheses.

## References to sources

From sections 5.11, 6.9, and 6.13:

- if sources are used, list bibliographic information in the source list;
- in-text references use the source number in square brackets;
- the source list follows the order of first appearance in the text and is numbered with Arabic numerals.

For short student labs, omit the bibliography only when no outside sources are cited or the instructor explicitly says to omit it.

## Contents and appendices

- a contents page may be omitted for a report of 10 pages or less;
- appendices are placed after the main report when needed and must be referenced from the text;
- appendices use continuous page numbering with the report.

## Title page

GOST 7.32-2001 defines a formal NIR title page with organization, work/report information, responsible persons, place, and date. Student laboratory reports commonly use a university-local title page rather than every NIR registration field. The skill therefore keeps the university title-page layout as an explicit institutional adaptation while preserving the general role/order of organization, work title, executors, reviewer, city, and year.
